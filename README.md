# Weakness? Resistance?
These are terms taken from the Pokemon TCG. Here's every DTCG weakness and resistance.

Holy: Weak to Creative, resists Chaos.
Electric: Weak to Virus, resists Order.
Virus: Weak to Flower, resists Holy.
Flower: Weak to Fighting, resists Electric.
Fighting: Weak to Holy, resists Fun.
Creative: Weak to Electric, resists Virus.
Fun: Weak to Order, resists Flower.
Chaos: Weak to Fun, resists Creative.
Order: Weak to Chaos, resists Fighting.
